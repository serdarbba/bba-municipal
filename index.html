Software for municipalities.

Three systems, built for two municipalities: coastal monitoring in Dubai, bay pollution monitoring and excavation tracking in İzmir. All of them turn imagery the municipality already produces into a dated record it can be held to.

01 · Dubai Municipality — in delivery

Coastal monitoring

Drone control and image processing

Two parts, plainly. We control the flight, and we read what comes back. A single survey flight over the coast returns fifteen separate answers automatically, with a dated report at the end, running on the municipality's own servers.

  • Visitor counting and density by zone, safe-capacity breach
  • Vehicles in non-permitted areas, unauthorised boats, restricted-zone entry
  • Litter hotspots, water quality anomalies, vegetation damage
  • Shoreline position and erosion between two dated surveys
  • Rip current risk, swimmers in unsafe zones
  • Beach asset inventory and condition

Measured commitments agreed in the tender: people counting within ±10%, intrusion recall above 90%, fire above 85%, shoreline position within three metres.

02 & 03 · İzmir Metropolitan Municipality

Built

Bay pollution monitoring

Every vessel in the bay tracked from AIS, every coastal camera watched for discharge and anomaly. When something is released into the water, the system already knows which vessels were there, at what speed, and what they were carrying.

  • Live vessel picture from AIS, with per-vessel risk scoring
  • Coastal cameras read automatically for vessels and surface anomalies
  • Incident log with time, location and evidence attached
  • Runs on the municipality's own servers
Built

Excavation tracking, mobile

Water, gas and electricity open the same streets and the record of who opened what, when, and whether it was restored properly lives in a folder of photographs nobody can search. A field application records it at the point of work, and the difference between two dates is measured from imagery.

  • Mobile application for the field team, used at the excavation itself
  • New excavation detected and dated from repeat imagery
  • Restoration compared against the condition before the work
  • A record the coordination office can query

Also available to a municipality

Public space

One operating picture

Cameras, access points, alarms and patrols on one screen that classifies what it sees, ranks it and sends the nearest unit. Built for a 303,000 m² development in Dubai, applied the same way to a district. Works with the cameras already installed.

Practical

No new hardware, no obligation to fly

Most of this runs on imagery the municipality already produces: existing cameras, contractor photographs taken for progress payments, surveys already being flown. Where flights are needed they are carried out by the municipality or by a licensed local operator. We supply the software and the reporting.
